The agent that can explain why

Ask about your body. Get the why, with a source for every claim.

General, basic guidance from trusted public health sites, with a link behind every claim. It reads your own sleep and activity, remembers what you tried last time, and tells you when to see a doctor. It never diagnoses.

The problem

Health answers sound sure. Few show their work.

People type symptoms into a search bar or a chatbot and get either a wall of worst cases or a confident paragraph with no sources. Neither one looks at how they actually slept, and neither one remembers what they tried last week.

  • No reasonsAnswers rarely say why a cause fits, or how to tell it apart from the others.
  • No evidenceClaims come without a page, a study or the person's own numbers behind them.
  • No memoryEvery chat starts from zero, so nothing builds on what worked or didn't.

How it works

Nine steps, all shown to the user

The app shows each step as it happens: the safety check, what it looked up, what it remembered and which AI model wrote the answer. Nothing is hidden.

  1. Danger signs first

    Chest pain, stroke signs, self-harm and similar go straight to emergency help, with no model call.

    Safety filter

  2. Questions first

    When the safe answer depends on a missing detail, up to three tap-to-answer questions come first.

    Nemotron

  3. Possible causes

    Your words become up to four standard condition names. They are search terms, not a diagnosis.

    Nemotron Bolt

  4. Trusted pages + your week

    1,016 MedlinePlus topics, MyHealthfinder action guides, NIH herb pages and your Fitbit week.

    Zetaris

  5. Live research

    PubMed is searched at question time for a recent systematic review on each cause.

    PubMed

  6. Recall past check-ins

    What you asked and tried before, and when, so the advice builds on it.

    Meterless H-MEM

  7. Answer with sources

    Possible causes, what to do now, what the research says and when to see a doctor. Each point has a small tag you can tap to see where it came from: a health page, your own sleep data or a past check-in.

    Nightshift + Nemotron

  8. Check every line

    Lines without a source are matched by meaning to a source sentence, or labelled "no source". Every decision is logged.

    Check-In

  9. Remember it

    The check-in is saved with its sources, so next week's check-in can ask how it went.

    Meterless H-MEM

Sponsor technology

What each piece does

Data layer

Zetaris

All health sources and the user's Fitbit log are tables in one Zetaris database, LYFE_HEALTH_CHECKIN: user_log, medlineplus, myhealthfinder, nutrient_limits and pubmed.

The agent looks them up through the Zetaris Lightning API, and the app shows the exact query it sent.

Memory layer

Meterless H-MEM

The open Meterless H-MEM engine stores each check-in and each "what I tried" note with a source, a confidence score and the time it was learned. Every change is logged and can't be erased, so the agent can always show why it remembers something.

Before answering, the agent recalls past check-ins, so it can say "last time you went to bed earlier, and your sleep went from 5.7 to 6.3 hours".

Model hub

Meterless Nightshift

Nightshift runs on a Windows PC on the same network in Hotspot mode, and gives the agent one address to reach the model. The agent never holds the NVIDIA key.

Swapping models, or going fully local with Ollama, is one setting.

Reasoning

NVIDIA Nemotron

Nemotron 3.5 Bolt turns the person's words into condition names in under a second. Nemotron 3 Ultra writes the cited answer in about 15 seconds.

Only the weekly summary leaves the computer. The raw log stays local.

Safe by design

Helpful, never a doctor

No diagnosisIt weighs possible causes and says how to tell them apart. Medicines appear only as options to ask a doctor about.
Danger signs skip adviceEmergency symptoms go straight to emergency help before any model call.
Private by defaultYour raw log stays on your computer. Each demo visitor gets their own memory.

Try it yourself

The live demo runs on the builder's own computers: Zetaris and H-MEM on Linux, and Nightshift with Nemotron on a Windows laptop. If it is offline, the screenshots above and the code on GitHub show everything it does.
