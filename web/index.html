Why I built this

Keeping your health in check, between checkups.

Sourced answers, and a heads-up when it's time to see a doctor.

We've all had that moment: something feels off, and you're not sure what it is or whether it's worth a doctor's visit. I once waited twelve hours in an emergency room and went home with advice to drink honey, lemon and ginger tea. I wished I'd had a trusted way to understand what was going on, and when to worry.

Check-In never diagnoses and never replaces a doctor. It explains what something could be, what you can safely do, and when to see a doctor, with a source behind every point.

What makes it different

Every point has a source

Causes, steps and warning signs link to MedlinePlus, MyHealthfinder, NCCIH or a PubMed review. Anything no source supports is left out of the answer.

It starts from your data

It reads your own Fitbit week and suggests what to ask, with the facts behind each idea, before you type a word.

It remembers and follows up

Each check-in and note is kept with its source, confidence and date, so next time it asks whether what you tried helped.

How it decides

Seven steps for every check-in. Each one is shown under "Show your work", with the reason behind each choice.

  1. Safety + NemotronDanger signs first

    Plain rules, then NVIDIA's Nemotron Safety Guard. Emergencies and harmful requests go straight to help.

  2. NVIDIA NemotronWords to medical terms

    A small model lists possible causes and any remedy you named, as search terms only.

  3. ZetarisTrusted library and your week

    SQL through the Zetaris Lightning API over 1,016 MedlinePlus topics, MyHealthfinder guides, NCCIH and your Fitbit log.

  4. PubMedLive research

    A recent systematic review for each possible cause. Case reports are skipped.

  5. Meterless H-MEMWhat you told it before

    Past check-ins recalled by meaning, each with a source, confidence and an append-only history.

  6. Meterless NightshiftThe answer, from evidence only

    NVIDIA Nemotron writes from the sources above, citing each point. Off-topic sources are dropped first.

  7. Check-InCheck every line

    Uncited lines are matched by meaning to a source sentence or removed, and every decision is logged.

Where it runs

Two machines on one home network. Meterless Nightshift is a Windows app, so it runs on a second laptop and serves NVIDIA Nemotron to the Linux machine that runs everything else.
